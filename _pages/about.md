Assoc. Prof. Dr. Berna Akgenc Hanedar completed her Ph.D. in the Department of Physics at Yıldız Technical University and her bachelor's degree in the Department of Physics at Kocaeli University. She began her academic career as a research assistant at Kırklareli University in 2010 and was awarded the title of Associate Professor on September 29, 2020. She is currently working as an Associate Professor at Kırklareli University. In 2009-2010, she went to Augsburg University to conduct her master's thesis research. Between 2013 and 2015, she completed her doctoral dissertation studies at Texas A&M University in the State of Texas, USA, and earned her Ph.D. degree in 2017. Between 2022 and 2023, she conducted postdoctoral research at Rice University in Texas, USA. Since 2023, she has also been working part-time as a research associate professor on an ERC project at Koç University, continuing her scientific studies.

Her research focuses on developing and applying the tools of theoretical and compu- tational physics for the proper understanding of the physical properties of objects in the nanoscale size range. She has addressed critical challenges in various areas in- cluding 2D materials, surface phenomena, nanostructures, magnetism, spintronics, and hydrogen storage from nano to macroscale using advanced computational sim- ulation methods. Using state-of-the-art techniques I investigate a broad range of topics of current interest in the field of material science and engineering.
